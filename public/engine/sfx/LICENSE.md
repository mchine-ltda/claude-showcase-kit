# Engine sound effects

All files in this folder are synthesised from scratch by `engine/audio/synth-sfx.ts`
(oscillators, filtered noise and an algorithmic reverb). They contain no samples or
recordings from third parties, so no external licence applies. They may be used in
client work, organic posts and paid advertising. Regenerate with `npm run showcase -- sfx`.

- `whoosh-soft.wav`: short air pass for text reveals and soft cuts
- `whoosh-long.wav`: longer pass for device entrances
- `impact-soft.wav`: low, soft landing thump for objects settling
- `riser.wav`: filtered build into the wordmark
- `chime.wav`: gentle glass bell for the agency signature
- `click.wav`: mouse click (press and release)
- `tap.wav`: muted touch tick
- `swipe.wav`: light airy scroll gesture
- `ambient-bed.wav`: optional evolving pad, loops every 16 s
